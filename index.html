Why Conventional Opaque LED Walls Fail Modern Architecture

As India’s Grade-A commercial real estate, transit infrastructure, and luxury retail sectors modernize, traditional black-cabinet LED screens create severe architectural bottlenecks: they block natural sunlight, require heavy structural steel retrofitting, trap heat against glazing, and violate municipal facade aesthetics.

Heurika bridges tier-1 global transparent LED invention with precision Indian field engineering. We work directly with master architects, facade consultants, and MEP engineers to integrate featherweight, see-through media systems directly behind existing curtain walls.

// PATENTED HARDWARE STANDARDS

Built on Foundational 3D-SMT™ Engineering

Heurika deploys systems sourced exclusively from original patent holders with >400KK monthly single-bin lamp production capacity, ensuring zero color shift across multi-thousand-square-foot facades.

01 / 8-STAGE SOLDERING

Patented 3D-SMT™ Technology

Every PCB soldering plate undergoes a rigorous 8-stage surface treatment. Side-mounted LED lamps withstand up to 40N (±4kg) of mechanical force before breaking loose.

02 / THERMAL CONDUCTIVITY

Uniform Copper Heat Dissipation

Mounting LED lamps on the PCB edge with internal conductive copper channels guarantees rapid heat transfer, evenly distributing IC load and preventing color aberration.

03 / OPTICAL CONTRAST

Matte Encapsulation & All-Black Housing

Single-bin, single-batch outdoor-grade lamps with anti-UV matte encapsulation and all-black housing deliver superior contrast ratios even against bright sky reflections.
